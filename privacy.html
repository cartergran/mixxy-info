Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Overview

Mixxy ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Mixxy mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

Account and Profile Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and a username. You may also provide a neighborhood and profile photo. Your username is permanent and cannot be changed after registration.

Activity Data

We store the actions you take within the App: activities you swipe on, like, star, or add to playbooks; playbooks you create; ratings and notes you add to activities. This data is stored on your account and is used to power your Library and personalize your Discover feed.

Photos You Upload

You can choose to upload photos of an activity from your photo library. Photos you upload are always visible to you and are reviewed by us before anyone else can see them. Approved photos are shown to other users unless you turn off "Share my photos publicly" when editing your profile, in which case they remain visible only to you. Other users can report a photo for review.

Shared Playbooks

If you choose to share a playbook, we create a link to it. Anyone who has that link can see the playbook's name, the activities in it, and your username. A playbook is not shared unless you create a link.

Analytics Data

We use PostHog to collect usage data, including which features you interact with and general usage patterns. This helps us improve the App. While you are signed in, this data is associated with your account identifier (a random ID), not with your name, email address, or username. PostHog data is not sold to third parties.

Advertising Data

Mixxy displays ads through Google AdMob. On iOS, we ask for your permission (via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt) before accessing your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). If you grant permission, your IDFA may be used to deliver personalized ads. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized. You can change this permission at any time in your device Settings under Privacy and Security.

How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and maintain your account and Library
  • To personalize your Discover feed by excluding activities you have already seen
  • To improve App features and fix bugs using aggregated analytics
  • To display advertisements that support free access to the App
  • To respond to support requests you send to us

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your data to build profiles for sale to advertisers.

Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party services, each of which has its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase (supabase.com) — database, authentication, and file storage. Your account data, activity data, profile photo, and uploaded activity photos are stored on Supabase infrastructure.
  • PostHog (posthog.com) — product analytics. Collects usage events associated with your account identifier.
  • Google AdMob (admob.google.com) — in-app advertising. May collect your device advertising identifier with your consent, as described above.
  • Resend (resend.com) — email delivery. When you upload or report a photo, a notification containing your email address and account identifier is sent to our support team so we can review it.

Data Retention

We retain your account and activity data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account in the App, your account, profile, and activity data are permanently deleted. Your profile photo and any uploaded photos that have not been published to other users are also removed. Photos you contributed that have already been published to other users remain in Mixxy but are no longer linked to you. Notification emails we received when you uploaded or reported a photo, which include your email address, may remain in our support inbox. Analytics data collected before you delete your account is not deleted; it remains associated with an account identifier that no longer corresponds to any account.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at mixxysupport@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and correction: You can view and update your profile information within the App at any time.
  • Account deletion: You can permanently delete your account at any time from the Profile tab by tapping Delete account. If you cannot sign in, email mixxysupport@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.
  • Advertising preferences: You can withdraw consent for personalized ads in your device Settings under Privacy and Security at any time.

Security

We use industry-standard security practices, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and row-level security policies on our database, to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, contact us at mixxysupport@gmail.com.
